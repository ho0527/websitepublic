How to Play Star Battle

  1. Move the spaceship using the sensible areas in the screen.
  2. The timer present the time lapsed.
  3. The fuel counter show the remain fuel.
  4. During the flight, destroy the asteroids and the enemy spaceships.
  5. You can shoot pressing Space bar.
  6. Hitting an asteroid or a spaceship costs 15 points of fuel.
  7. Enemy spaceship needs 1 shot to be destroyed: +5 points.
  8. Asteroid needs 2 shots to be destroyed: +10 points.
  9. If you destroy a friendly spaceship you lose 10 points.
  10. During the flight, collect the fuel in the space.
  11. Pause the game with the pause button or the letter P.
  12. Go beyond all limits!
