Parc de la Tete d'Or opened in 1857 and is still one of the largest urban parks in France.

Its name comes from a legend claiming that a golden head of Christ was buried somewhere under the grounds.

tete-dor-lake.jpeg

The lake covers sixteen hectares and can be crossed by rowing boat during the summer months.

The botanical garden shelters greenhouses from the nineteenth century, and the rose garden holds more than thirty thousand bushes.
