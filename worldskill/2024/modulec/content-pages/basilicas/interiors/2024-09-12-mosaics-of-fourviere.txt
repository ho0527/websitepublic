The upper church of Fourviere is covered from floor to vault with mosaics made of gold leaf, coloured glass and Italian marble.

Six large panels face each other in pairs, three on the history of France and three on the history of the Church.

fourviere-mosaic.jpeg

Every panel was assembled tessera by tessera by the Lyon workshop of Georges Decote, who needed almost twenty years to complete the set.

This page is stored inside a nested sub-folder, basilicas/interiors, so it also demonstrates the nested routing rule.
