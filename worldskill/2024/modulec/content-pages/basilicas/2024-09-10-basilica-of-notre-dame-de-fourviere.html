--- title: Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourviere tags: Lyon, France, Religion, UNESCO cover: fourviere.jpeg summary: The white basilica on the hill that prays, visible from almost every street of the city. ---

Perched on Fourviere hill, the basilica was built between 1872 and 1884 as a thank-you for the city being spared during the Franco-Prussian war.

Its four octagonal towers and the gilded Virgin on the bell tower make it the most recognisable silhouette of Lyon.

Getting there

The funicular from Vieux Lyon climbs the hill in two minutes, but the Rosary garden path rewards walkers with the best panorama of the Presqu'ile.
