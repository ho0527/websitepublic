--- title: Lyon's Majestic Crown: The Cathedral of Saint-Jean-Baptiste tags: Lyon, France, Art, Museum cover: saint-jean-baptiste.jpeg summary: Standing at the foot of Fourviere hill, the Cathedral of Saint-Jean-Baptiste has crowned Old Lyon for eight centuries. ---

Hey history buffs, art enthusiasts, and anyone with a love for medieval stonework! Prepare to be amazed by the Cathedral of Saint-Jean-Baptiste, a building that took nearly three hundred years to finish and still keeps a few secrets.

This captivating monument blends Romanesque foundations with soaring Gothic windows, and it sits right in the middle of the UNESCO listed Vieux Lyon district.

Here's why a visit to the cathedral is a must-do:

  • A Feast for the Eyes: Gaze in awe at the thirteenth century stained glass of the apse, still holding its original deep blues and rubies.
  • A Glimpse into History: The cathedral hosted two church councils and the wedding of Henry IV, so the flagstones have carried a lot of famous footsteps.
  • Astronomical Magic: The fourteenth century astronomical clock rings out at fixed hours and still tracks feast days more than six hundred years later.
  • Interactive Exhibits: The neighbouring treasury museum lets you get close to liturgical objects that are usually locked away.

Come at dusk, when the floodlights pick out the carved portals, and you will understand why the people of Lyon call this their majestic crown.
