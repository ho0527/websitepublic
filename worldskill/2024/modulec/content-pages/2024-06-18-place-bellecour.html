Place Bellecour: The Heart of Lyon

Place Bellecour stretches over 62,000 square metres of red gravel between the Rhone and the Saone, which makes it the largest open pedestrian square in Europe.

The equestrian statue of Louis XIV in the centre is the reference point every citizen of Lyon uses when giving directions.

The square has no title in its front-matter, so the rendered title has to be taken from the first h1 element of this file.

Come in December and the whole square turns into the main stage of the Fete des Lumieres.
