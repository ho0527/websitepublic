At the very tip of the peninsula, where the Rhone and the Saone finally merge, stands a building that looks like a cloud resting on a plinth of glass.

The Musee des Confluences opened in December 2014 after a construction saga that lasted more than a decade.

confluences.jpeg

Inside, natural history, anthropology and the sciences share the same floor, so a mammoth skeleton can face a Polynesian canoe without anyone finding it strange.

The permanent exhibition is organised around four questions: where do we come from, who are we, what do we do, and what happens after us.

lyon-skyline.jpeg

Come at the end of the day, when the setting sun turns the stainless steel skin into a mirror of the two rivers.
