How EnviroFund works

Three steps, no middlemen

Instead of a handful of large investors, thousands of people finance a project with a few euros each. 100 % of your contribution reaches the project team.

  1. 01

    Pick a project

    Filter by cause, country or how urgent the deadline is. Every page shows exactly what your money buys.

  2. 02

    Fund it in 30 seconds

    From €3 upwards. You are only charged when the project reaches its target amount.

  3. 03

    Watch it happen

    Teams post photo updates every month, and you can ask them anything in the comments.
